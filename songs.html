My Favorite Songs 🎵

Expectation – Olivia Rodrigo feat. Chappell Roan

This song captures the raw emotions of growing up and facing expectations. Olivia’s voice, combined with Chappell Roan’s unique style, makes it powerful and relatable. It reminds me of the struggles of balancing school, personal dreams, and the pressure to succeed.

What I love most is how the lyrics feel like a conversation with yourself questioning whether you’re living up to what others want, or following your own path. It’s a reminder that even when life feels overwhelming, it’s okay to slow down and breathe. This track motivates me to keep pushing forward, even when I doubt myself.

NYMPHOLOGY – Melanie Martinez

Melanie’s creativity shines here whimsical yet haunting. The lyrics explore identity and self-expression, which resonates with me as someone still discovering my place in the world. Her artistry inspires me to embrace uniqueness and creativity.

Listening to this song feels like stepping into a dream world. The playful yet eerie vibe makes me reflect on how society often tries to label us, but we have the power to define ourselves. It’s a track that reminds me to stay true to who I am, even if it means standing out.

My Immortal (Band Version) – Evanescence

This classic is pure emotion and resilience. The haunting melody and powerful vocals remind me that even in moments of sadness, there’s strength in vulnerability. It’s a song I turn to when I need comfort or motivation to keep going.

What makes this track timeless is its ability to connect with deep feelings we often hide. The band version adds intensity, making the emotions even stronger. It’s not just a sad song it’s a reminder that pain can be transformed into beauty, and that music has the power to heal. Every time I listen, I feel a sense of release and renewal.
